# Dancing

Spontaneous group activity — daytime counterpart to nighttime shanty singing.

## Trigger conditions (autonomous)
- Daytime (bright)
- World-level dance cooldown expired
- Member not in conversation cooldown
- High average crew morale
- Random chance per tick
- Multiple idle/wandering humans on same deck

## Mechanics
- Timed activity with morale and friendship gains on completion
- Dancing skill increases over time while dancing
- World-level cooldown prevents back-to-back dances
- Conversation cooldown set after dancing (prevents immediate chatter)

## Visuals
- Thought bubble: `music_note` (reused from singing)
- State indicator glyph: ♫ (U+266B, double music note — distinct from singing's ♪)
- Dancers oscillate back and forth, spin around, sometimes moonwalk
- Activity log: "{name} led a merry dance"

## Grouping
Reuses `shantyInitiatorId` field — crew can't sing and dance simultaneously (different time-of-day triggers).

## Command
`Dance` (no args) — gathers nearby idle humans and starts dancing. Requires at least 2 dancers.
Available via order files and command shorthand.

## No audio
Dancing is silent for now. Can be added later.
