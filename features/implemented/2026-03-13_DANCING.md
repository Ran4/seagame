# Dancing

Daytime counterpart to nighttime shanty singing. Can be solo or group.

## Trigger conditions (autonomous)
- Daytime (bright)
- World-level dance cooldown expired
- Member not in conversation cooldown
- High average crew morale
- Random chance per tick
- Multiple idle/wandering humans on same deck

Autonomous dancing is always group dancing.

## Mechanics
- Timed activity with morale gain on completion
- Group dance also gives friendship gains among dancers
- Dancing skill increases over time while dancing
- World-level cooldown prevents back-to-back group dances
- Conversation cooldown set after dancing (prevents immediate chatter)

## Visuals
- Thought bubble: `music_note` (reused from singing)
- State indicator glyph: ♫ (U+266B, double music note — distinct from singing's ♪)
- Three dance moves cycle: stepping (back and forth), spinning in place (pause → spin → pause), and moonwalking (move one way, face the other)
- Activity log (group): "{name} led a merry dance"

## Grouping
Reuses `shantyInitiatorId` field — crew can't sing and dance simultaneously (different time-of-day triggers).

## Commands
- `Dance` (no args) — solo dance. No group needed, no world cooldown.
- `GroupDance` (no args) — gathers nearby idle humans. Requires at least 2 dancers. Sets world cooldown.

Both available via order files and command shorthand.

## No audio
Dancing is silent for now. Can be added later.
