# Fishing

Low-stakes, meditative activity to balance against combat and storms.

Solves the food supply problem (currently crew eat at the stove with infinite food), creates a new idle behavior, and adds another reason to care about where you sail.

## Design

### Fishing Mechanic
- New tile type: FISHING_SPOT (railing tiles on upper deck).
- Crew ordered to fish walk to the spot and cast a line.
- Fish bite after a certain time (random; if 2026-03-12_CREW_SKILLS.md is implemented use a system based on
  it, otherwise leave a markdown file in issues/ remembering us that we need to add it once crew skills is implemented and in the meanwhile just use randomly 15-60 seconds). Caught fish go to barrel inventory as food items.

### Fish Variety
- Common fish (+20 hunger) — everywhere.
- Tropical fish (+30 hunger, sell at harbors) — near warm islands.
- Swordfish (+50 hunger, rare) — deep water.
- Pufferfish (poisonous — +50 hunger, but eating gives "sick" status for 1 in-game day, crew vomits and can't work).

### Autonomous Behavior
- Idle crew with low ship food supplies autonomously choose to fish (like they choose to eat when hungry).
- Creates a self-sustaining crew economy.

### Food Spoilage Tie-in
- Fish spoil faster than preserved rations.
- The existing `spoilAfter` infrastructure just needs to be enforced (see 2026-03-10_ITEM_SPOILAGE.md).
- Creates a supply chain: catch fish → eat soon or it spoils → need to fish again or buy preserved food.

## Implementation Notes

New TileType FISHING_SPOT, new CrewState FISHING, new item types for fish.
Follow existing patterns: add to TileType enum, WALKABLE set, TILE_COLORS, CHAR_TO_TILE, deck ASCII layout, TILE_NAMES, TILE_ACTIONS.

The idle behavior follows the standard pattern in updateIdleHuman().
