Feature Roadmap

What's done and what's on the horizon for Sea Game

On the Horizon

Planned features, coming soon

Monkey Mischief

Monkeys steal items from barrels and crew, relocating them around the ship. Chase them down before your grog vanishes into the rigging!

Puppies & Babies

Dogs and monkeys can breed and birth litters of tiny babies that follow their mothers around the ship. Watch them grow from wobbly infants to full-grown adults over time.

Crew Skills

Crew members level up Sailing, Gunnery, Combat, Cooking, and Navigation through practice. High-level sailors unlock special abilities — making every veteran irreplaceable.

Fishing

A chill fishing mechanic where crew cast lines for different fish types. Hungry sailors will autonomously fish when food supplies run low — a nice break between cannon fire.

Harbor Towns

Islands become bustling ports with taverns, markets, and recruitment offices. Buy supplies, hire sailors, gamble in bar fights, and gather intel — each port with its own personality.

Sea Monsters

Face krakens and leviathans in epic multi-phase encounters. Crew must hack tentacles and hold their nerve — low-morale sailors may freeze. Victory means legendary loot.

Ship-to-Ship Combat

The core action loop: broadside cannon duels, hull breaches with flooding, and boarding parties. Crew effectiveness depends on hunger and morale — every system feeds into the fight.

Storms & Weather

Dynamic storms roll in with howling winds, tearing sails, and sliding cargo. Crew stumble on wet decks, and lightning strikes can spark fires. Low-morale sailors may refuse orders entirely.

Treasure Maps

Find or purchase weathered maps pointing to buried treasure on distant islands. Send shore parties on multi-hour expeditions — they may return with gold, cursed relics, or rescued castaways.

Drunk Fighting

Two sloshed sailors who don't like each other? Expect fists. Drunk crew with low friendship will throw punches, earning bruises and tanking their relationship further. Pure comedy.

Morale, Shanties & Mutiny

Crew morale rises and falls with hunger, rest, and friendships. Happy sailors sing shanties together at night. Push them too far and mutiny brews — 60% angry crew means game over.

Item Spoilage

Food and perishables decay over time in barrels and inventories. Keep an eye on spoilage timers or watch your provisions turn rotten. Items grey out as they near expiry.
