By lantern glow we trimmed the sail where the black tide kissed the quay,
With powder dry and iron warm we laughed at fate and sea,
The wind it bit, the gulls cried out, the night ran cold and bleak,
Then Powderwake came thundering on with fire along her keel.

Oh, Powderwake, roll on, roll on, shake the foam and spray,
Beat the drum and haul the line, we’ll steal the break of day,
Oh, Powderwake, sing low, sing loud, through smoke and storm and gale,
We’ll ride your wake till dawn awakes and victory fills the sail.
