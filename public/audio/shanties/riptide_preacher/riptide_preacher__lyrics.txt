The riptide preacher stood barefoot on the foam,
With a Bible full of thunder and a voice like splintered oak,
He cried, “You can’t outrun the hunger of the sea,”
But we laughed and hauled the mainsail through the smoke.

Oh, riptide preacher, sing us where we’re bound,
Through the black-toothed breakers and the devil’s grinding sound,
If we drown, we drown with salt upon our tongues,
And your ragged gospel roaring from the swells.
