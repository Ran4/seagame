Cold dawn crack, the riggin' groans, shiverin_ropes in the spray,
Bootheels drum on the salted planks, and the gulls all wheel away.
Heave, mates, heave, through the iron wind we drive,
With tar on our hands and a curse on our lips, we keep this hull alive.

Oh, shiverin_ropes, sing low, sing wild,
Carry us home through the black-tossed tide.
Oh, shiverin_ropes, pull true, pull tight,
We'll laugh at the storm by the lantern light.
