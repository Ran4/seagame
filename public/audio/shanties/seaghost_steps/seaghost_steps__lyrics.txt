At dawn we heard the seaghost steps come tapping on the deck,
A salt-cold drum of hollow heels that made the rigging wreck,
No face beneath the dripping hood, no shadow in the sun,
Just whispers through the lantern smoke: your tide has just begun.

Hey, ho, the seaghost steps, they rattle through the foam,
Hey, ho, keep tight the line, for no lost soul sails home,
Stamp the boards and face the gale, let no brave heart forget,
The sea keeps time with seaghost steps, and we are dancing yet.
